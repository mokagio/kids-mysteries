Nibbles is the Class 3 hamster. His cage sits on the windowsill, next to the plant shelf.

On **Friday afternoon** Class 3 said goodnight to him and went home.
On **Monday morning** the cage door was open and Nibbles was gone.

Only three grown-ups went into Class 3 over the weekend.
Two of them are telling the truth.
Read the {{< word def="things you can see or find that help you work out what really happened" >}}evidence{{< /word >}} and work out who is fibbing.

{{< suspect name="Nora Blitt" role="The Cleaner" emoji="🪣" >}}
"I shampooed the carpet on Saturday morning. I never went near the cage."
{{< /suspect >}}

{{< suspect name="Mr. Otis Crane" role="The Music Teacher" emoji="🎸" >}}

{{< /suspect >}}

{{< suspect name="Wendy Marsh" role="The Gardener" emoji="🌱" >}}

{{< /suspect >}}

{{< /suspects >}}

{{< clues >}}

1. Nora shampooed the carpet backwards, from the far wall to the door, the way cleaners do so they never tread on the wet part.
2. The carpet stayed damp until Monday. Anyone who walked on it left footprints.
3. There were two sets of footprints, and only two. The first went to the cupboard at the back, then straight back to the door.
4. The second went to the plant shelf, then along to the windowsill, then out of the door.
5. A line of sunflower seeds led from the windowsill out to the garden shed. There were sunflower seeds in the bottom of Wendy's watering can.

{{< /clues >}}

{{< solution culprit="Wendy Marsh" >}}

Nora never walked on the wet carpet at all.
She shampooed her way backwards to the door, so she left no footprints — and she could not have reached the cage without leaving some.

The first set of footprints goes to the cupboard at the back and straight out again.
That is Mr. Crane fetching his guitar. He never went near the window.

There were only two sets, so nobody else came in.

The second set goes to the plant shelf, and then along to the windowsill.
Only one of them watered the plants. Those are Wendy's footprints — and she said Nibbles was still in his cage when she left.

The seeds finish it.
They lead from the windowsill out to the shed, and there are seeds in the bottom of her watering can.
She carried Nibbles out in it, with a scoop of his food so he would not be hungry.

Wendy thought Nibbles looked cold by the window.
Her {{< word def="the reason someone had for doing it" >}}motive{{< /word >}} was a kind one.
She meant to bring him back on Monday, and then she was too shy to say.

Nibbles is fine. He is fast asleep in the shed, in a nest of grass clippings.
