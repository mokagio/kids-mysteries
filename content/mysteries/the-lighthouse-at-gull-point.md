Captain Sloat was found at the top of his lighthouse, 199 steps up, beside a brass telescope with a dent in it.
The great lamp had stopped turning at exactly **9:40 pm** — he was killed while winding it.

The lighthouse stands on a rock, joined to the shore by a stone causeway that floods at high tide.

{{< clues >}}

1. The causeway was underwater from 9:00 pm until 11:00 pm. Nobody crossed it on foot during that time.
2. Crumb's bicycle and empty post-bag were found on the *mainland* side of the causeway.
3. The harbourmaster's log records Pike's boat lamp out among the lobster pots, half a mile off, at 9:40 pm.
4. The kitchen door bolts from the outside only. Mrs. Vane was still bolted in at midnight.
5. The stair handrail was painted green that morning and was still wet. A green smear was found on the strap of Dr. Quill's binoculars.
6. Dr. Quill's notebook has entries at 10:00 and 10:30 pm describing the lighthouse beam sweeping over her tent.

{{< /clues >}}

{{< solution culprit="Dr. Ada Quill" >}}

Crumb's bicycle was on the far side of the flooded causeway, so he was already back on the mainland before 9:00 and couldn't return.
Pike was half a mile out to sea at the moment of the murder.
Mrs. Vane was bolted in from the outside — she couldn't have bolted herself in, so she never left that kitchen.

Only Dr. Quill was loose on the rock. The wet green paint says she climbed the stairs.

And her notebook seals it: she wrote about the beam sweeping over her tent at 10:00 and 10:30 — but the lamp stopped turning at 9:40. She was inventing an alibi for hours she couldn't account for.

{{< /solution >}}
