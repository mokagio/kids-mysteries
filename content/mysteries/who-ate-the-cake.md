---
title: Who Ate the Cake?
date: 2026-08-06
setting: Bramble Lane School
missing: One big slice of chocolate cake

---

Miss Plum baked a chocolate cake for sports day.
She left it on the table in the staff room at **twelve o'clock**.

When she came back at **one o'clock**, a big slice was gone.

Three people were in school at lunch time.
Each one says they were somewhere else.
That is called an {{< word def="a reason why you could not have done it, because you were somewhere else at the time" >}}alibi{{< /word >}}.

Two of them are telling the truth.

{{< suspects >}}

{{< suspect name="Bert Dunn" role="The Caretaker" emoji="🧹" >}}
"I was up a ladder all lunch time, painting the gym."
{{< /suspect >}}

{{< suspect name="Rosa Kite" role="The Art Teacher" emoji="🎨" >}}
"I was in the art room with my class. We were making dragons."
{{< /suspect >}}

{{< suspect name="Coach Tibbs" role="The Sports Teacher" emoji="⚽" >}}
"I was out on the field with the running team, twelve till one."
{{< /suspect >}}

{{< /suspects >}}

{{< clues >}}

1. There was sticky chocolate icing on the staff room door handle.
2. Bert had wet white paint on his hands all lunch time. There was no white paint on the handle.
3. Rosa's class stayed with her the whole hour. Twenty-eight children say she never left the room.
4. The running team went home at half past twelve, not at one o'clock.
5. A whistle on a red string was under the staff room table. It had icing on it.

{{< /clues >}}

{{< solution culprit="Coach Tibbs" >}}

Bert had wet paint on his hands, and there was no paint on the door handle.
So Bert never opened that door.

Rosa never left the art room. Twenty-eight children saw her stay.

That leaves Coach Tibbs.
He said he was on the field until one o'clock, but the running team went home at half past twelve.
So he had half an hour that he did not tell anyone about.

And his whistle was under the table, with icing on it.
That is the last piece of {{< word def="things you can see or find that help you work out what really happened" >}}evidence{{< /word >}}, and it makes Coach Tibbs the {{< word def="the person who did it" >}}culprit{{< /word >}}.

{{< /solution >}}
